Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my clients or team need to install anything?

No! That is the core advantage of MyRemotePC. The host runs our lightweight standalone executable, while connecting clients simply open a web browser on any phone, tablet, Mac, or PC.

How does the 1-click Windows installer work?

The Windows setup installer (MyRemotePC-Setup-v1.0.0.exe) is completely self-contained. It installs to your user profile without needing administrator elevation, places icons on your desktop, and can auto-start with Windows.

Can I connect over the internet when away from home?

Yes. We include a 1-click share_over_internet.bat script that utilizes encrypted Cloudflare or Ngrok tunnels, giving you a secure public URL with zero port forwarding required on your router.

Is my session encrypted and secure?

Yes. All sessions require a PBKDF2-hashed access code, protected by active rate limiting to prevent brute force attacks, with direct peer streaming.
